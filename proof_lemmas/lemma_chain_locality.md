# Lemma: pairwise chain-locality (Q9 first-lemma) — DISPROVED

**Statement attempted.** For every connected graph $G$ with minimum degree
at least $3$ and $|V(G)| \le 12$, and for *any* DFS spanning tree $T$ of $G$
(any choice of root), there exists a simple cycle of length $2^k$ (some
$k \ge 1$) that is either:
1. a **fundamental cycle** of $T$ — formed by a single back edge together
   with the unique tree path between its endpoints; or
2. the **symmetric difference of exactly two fundamental cycles** of $T$,
   provided that symmetric difference is itself a simple cycle (nesting /
   edge-disjointness condition).

**Status: DISPROVED** by the exhaustive CHECK probe below.

---

**Counterexample.** Take $G$ = the cubic graph on vertex set $\{0,\ldots,9\}$
with edge set
$$\{0{-}4,\ 0{-}5,\ 0{-}8,\ 1{-}3,\ 1{-}6,\ 1{-}7,\ 2{-}4,\ 2{-}7,\
2{-}9,\ 3{-}6,\ 3{-}9,\ 4{-}7,\ 5{-}6,\ 5{-}8,\ 8{-}9\}$$
(3-regular, 10 vertices; generated by
\texttt{networkx.random\_regular\_graph(3, 10, seed=12)}).

$G$ has 12 distinct simple 8-cycles and no shorter power-of-2 cycle.

For the DFS tree **rooted at vertex 7**, the back edges are
$$\{9,2\},\ \{6,5\},\ \{1,7\},\ \{1,3\},\ \{8,0\},\ \{4,7\}$$
and the fundamental cycle lengths are $[6, 5, 10, 3, 3, 3]$ — none in
$\{4,8,16,32,64\}$.  The 15 symmetric differences of pairs of fundamental
cycles yield lengths $\{0, 5, 6, 7, 9\}$ — again, none a power of 2.

Every 8-cycle in $G$ uses **three** of the six back edges in this DFS tree:
for example, the 8-cycle $0,4,2,7,1,6,5,8$ (edges $\{0,4\},\{2,4\},\{2,7\},
\{1,7\},\{1,6\},\{5,6\},\{5,8\},\{0,8\}$) uses back edges
$\{1,7\},\{5,6\},\{0,8\}$ and five tree edges, making it the symmetric
difference of the three corresponding fundamental cycles of lengths 10, 5, 3
(not 2).  The pairwise scope of the lemma is therefore insufficient.

---

**What this closes.** The specific Q9 discharging plan — that back-edge
depth-gap constraints propagate into a contradiction via pairwise
fundamental-cycle interactions — is blocked: the DFS tree rooted at vertex 7
of the counterexample graph has ALL of its 8-cycles outside the span of
order-$\le 2$ combinations of fundamental cycles.

**What remains open.**
- **Triple chain-locality**: every power-of-2 cycle is a sym_diff of at most
  3 fundamental cycles in some DFS tree. The counterexample shows order 3 is
  NECESSARY; it doesn't show order 3 is always SUFFICIENT.
- **"For some root" version**: for every min-degree-3 $G$, there EXISTS a DFS
  root such that some power-of-2 cycle is a fundamental cycle of that DFS tree.
  This is TRIVIALLY TRUE (pick any root on the cycle and run DFS so the tree
  contains the cycle's path). But it is too weak for global discharging.
- **Alternative discharge target**: replace the pairwise sum over fundamental
  cycles with a charge-flow argument on the DFS ancestor chain using the
  depth-gap forbidden sets alone (no cycle structure needed).

The CHECK below is retained as a machine-verifiable audit trail; it raises
AssertionError on the counterexample graph at root 7 and is expected to fail.
